# Life Planner

A huge white board, like Miro, with faint grey dots and a light grey line at its edge. Log in from any device.

Opening the app goes straight to the board you opened last, with an **Open?** box on the right listing all your boards as big boxes that share out its space (the more boards, the smaller each box) so you can switch with one click. Click anywhere else or the x to close it. **Home** at the top left of a board goes to the Home page: your account email at the top left, a menu at the top right with the **Trash** (restore a board, or **Empty trash** to delete them all for good) and **Log out**, and all your boards grouped in the middle with no frame: white boxes that lift when you hover, each with a slightly blurred picture of what's on that board and its name on a scrap of torn paper in the top right corner (each board's tear is its own, with the pale fibres of the rip, a soft shadow and paper grain). Hovering a board shows **Remove** (moves it to the trash, where it can be restored for 30 days before it's deleted for good) and **Duplicate** (bottom right on Home, top right in Open?). On Home there's also **Merge**, which asks **All?** or **Select?**. All? picks another board, moves everything onto it (to the right of what's there) and puts this board in the trash. Select? opens the board with a blue edge: select what to merge, press **Continue**, pick the other board, and just those things move over. Before merging it says what will happen, and warns when the merged board may be slow because it's getting huge (over 1,000 things). If the other board was changed somewhere else meanwhile, nothing is merged and it asks to try again. Afterwards the other board opens. Both have **New board**, which opens the new board with the cursor in its name box, ready to type (Enter goes to the board). Three dots bounce while a board opens. Boards save by themselves as you work (the top left says Saved), and you can type over a board's name at the top left.

- Two-finger swipe on the trackpad moves around the board.
- Pinch on the trackpad, or the mouse scroll wheel, zooms in and out.
- Press **b** to add a circle at the cursor. It is selected straight away with its menu open.
- Press **t** to start typing text at the cursor straight away (the same text as the circle menu's Text, without making a circle first).
- Hold **n** to start a line at the cursor, move the mouse, and let go of **n** to confirm it. A line that starts or ends inside a circle or text is attached to it.
- Lines snap exactly level or upright when they are within a few degrees of horizontal or vertical (while drawing, dragging an end or dragging a bend).
- Click a line to show a dot on each end; drag a dot to move that end. Drag the middle of a line to move the whole line.
- Click a line and choose **Time**, then Day, Week, Month or Year. The line becomes a timeline, like a ruler. Stretch it longer on the board (its real length, not zooming) and the space between marks fills with smaller steps: dates in a month, months in a year, days in a week, hours in a day. The longer it is, the more show (every 15th date, then every 5th, then every date). A phase can start and end on these too; it then shows its ends in full, like "Nov 5" and "Nov 20".
  - **Day** (0–31), **Week** (1–4) and **Year**: two slots, Beginning → End. Type a number or scroll over a slot to count up or down. Enter moves from Beginning to End, and Enter again finishes.
  - **Month**: pick the beginning month (Jan–Dec), then the end month. It can wrap round the year, e.g. Nov → Feb.
- Click a timeline and a **Phase** box appears beside the first third of it (above a level timeline, left of an upright one, clear of the end dots). Press Phase, move along the timeline (the stretch shows in orange), click to set the start, then click again or press Enter to finish (Escape cancels). The stretch becomes a phase beside the original (on the right of an upright timeline, above a level one), no longer connected to it. A phase looks like a bracket "]": its ends curl toward the original timeline and it shows only its start and end dates, with no marks in between.
- Between Phase and Pin are colour dots that recolour the timeline (line, marks, labels and arrowheads).
- The same box has **Pin** (pin icon): press it, move along the timeline and click a mark. That mark becomes bigger and bold, and a small box opens with **B** (turn bold off or on) and colours. Click a pinned mark to open its box again.
- Click a line to show an arrow button beside each end. Click one to give that end an arrowhead; click again to remove it.
- Click a line and choose **Text** to type text in the middle of the line. Double-click that text to edit it.
- Click a line and choose **Angle** to add a bend point (a small diamond). Drag it to bend the line. Choose Angle again for more bends: the new one goes in the middle of the part of the line you clicked on (between the nearest bend and that tip). Click a bend and press Backspace or Delete to remove it; the line goes straight there.
- Click anything and press **Backspace** to delete it.
- **Ctrl+Z** (or Cmd+Z) undoes any change: adding, moving, resizing, recolouring, arrows, bends, deleting. **Ctrl+Shift+Z** (or Cmd+Shift+Z) redoes it.
- Drag on an empty part of the board to draw a selection box. Everything it touches is selected: drag any of them, or anywhere inside the dashed box, to move them all. Pinch (or mouse wheel) with the cursor inside the box resizes them all; a two-finger swipe, or anything outside the box, moves around the board as usual.
- With several things selected, click **Group** beside the selection box. Grouped things are selected and moved together; click **Ungroup** to split them again. Under that button is a row of colours: picking one gives every selected square, text and line that colour.
- Clicking a plain line (not a timeline) shows colours in its menu.
- With several things selected, the **Save preset** button in the top right saves them as a preset (Preset 1, Preset 2, ...). Presets belong to you, so they can be used on any board.
- The keys t, v, b, n and m can be changed in Home's menu (Shortcuts).
- Press **v** for view mode: nothing on the board can be changed, and hovering a text makes it bigger to read. A switch at the top shows it's on; press **v**, Escape or the switch to leave.
- Clicking a line shows thickness choices in its menu; selecting only lines shows them beside the selection box, for all of them at once.
- Press **m** to open the menu on the right (press **m** again to close it). Its **Preset** button drops down every saved preset with **Use** (places a copy in the middle of the screen), **Edit** (places it, and the top right button becomes **Update preset**: select what the preset should be and press it to save over the old one) and **Delete**.
- Click something (or select several) and press **Ctrl+C** (Cmd+C) to copy, then **Ctrl+V** (Cmd+V) to paste a copy at the cursor.
- A picture copied from outside the site (for example **Copy image** in a browser) pastes onto the board with **Ctrl+V** (Cmd+V). Drag it to move it; scroll or pinch while it's selected to resize it.
- A text dragged above or below a line snaps centred on the line's middle (pink guide).
- Something that overlaps something else shows **Layer** in its box: **Move to front** / **Move to back**.
- Drop a line end near a circle or text and it attaches: moving the circle or text moves that end. Drag the end away to detach it.
- Drag a circle to move it anywhere.
- Click a circle or text to select it and open its menu. While selected, the scroll wheel or trackpad resizes it instead of the board (text shows its px size and keeps its left edge in place). Click the board to deselect.
- The circle menu's **Text** turns the circle into text, ready to type. Enter (or clicking elsewhere) confirms; a space then Enter starts a new line. The text menu changes colour, bold and background fill (new texts have none). Above **Bold**, the checklist, bullet (•) and **1.2.3.** buttons turn every line of the text into that kind of list (press again to turn it back). Typing **-** then a space at the start of a line makes a bullet point. Starting a new line from a checklist line starts the next line with a new checkbox, on a bullet line with a new bullet, and on a numbered line with the next number (2 after 1, and so on). Space then Enter on a line that only has the checkbox, bullet or number ends the list. Click a checkbox to tick it off; click again to untick it. While typing, highlight some words to change just those: **Ctrl+B** makes them bold, and a small bar above the text appears with a **B** button, colours, background fills, and **1.2.3.**, bullet and checklist buttons that turn the highlighted lines into that kind of list. (For a different size, make another text.) **Tab** moves the current line slightly to the right (press again to go further) and **Shift+Tab** moves it back; a checklist or numbered line keeps its indent on the next line. Double-click text to edit it.
- The circle menu's **Square** turns the circle into a square outline. Only the outline can be clicked or dragged, not the space inside. Its menu changes its colour and has **Text**, which adds a label on the outside of its top left corner in the same colour. It resizes like a circle, and when clicked its corners show handles: drag one to stretch it into any rectangle.
- Dragging a circle, square or text near another one snaps its edges or middle in line with the other's, with pink guide lines, so things line up neatly side by side. Lines have their own snapping: dragging a line, an end or a bend lines its ends, bends and timeline marks up with other lines' ends, bends and marks. This also works when moving a group or selection that has squares in it.
- **On a phone or tablet** a toolbar at the bottom stands in for the keys: **Circle** (like b) and **Text** (like t) add one in the middle of the screen, **Line** (like holding n) makes your next finger drag draw one line, plus **Undo**, **Redo**, **Delete** (the selected things) and **Presets** (like v). Tap things to select them and drag them with one finger; drag on empty board for a selection box. Two fingers work like a trackpad: move them to move around, pinch to zoom, and with something selected a pinch resizes it (a group only when you pinch inside its box).
- A text's **Link** (chain icon): hover it, then **Add to**, then pick **Today's to-do** or **Milestone** to add the text there as a task (hover **Milestone** to pick one of its task pages instead). The text gets a checkbox that's ticked together with the task; removing either one leaves the other. (In other menus the chain icon does nothing yet.)

### Home

- **Today's to-do** (top left): click it to start typing; Enter adds the next line. It starts empty every day (a note says so while you hover it); the archive icon opens **Past to-dos**, the last 30 days.
- **Milestone**: click its dates to pick Day / Week / Month / Year and a beginning and end (Enter or click away to set), then write its tasks underneath. Hover a task (or tap it on a phone) for the arrow that opens the task's own page for notes and checklists. On that page, hovering a checklist line (or tapping it on a phone) shows **Add to today's to-do?**, which adds it to today's list, ticked together.
- The faint line above the boards counts the days until the milestone ends.
- Hover a board for the little eye in its corner to hide it. The eye button beside **New board** (on the right) shows the hidden boards underneath; press a hidden board's eye to put it back in its spot.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Supabase (login). Deployed on Vercel.

## Setup

### 1. Supabase (login and database)

1. Create a free project at [supabase.com](https://supabase.com).
2. Open **SQL Editor → New query**, paste everything in [`supabase/schema.sql`](supabase/schema.sql) and click **Run**.
3. Open **Project Settings → API** (or the **Connect** button) and copy the **Project URL** and the **anon / publishable key**.

### 2. Run it on your computer (optional)

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env.local   # then paste your two Supabase values in
npm run dev
```

Open http://localhost:3000.

### 3. Put it online with Vercel

1. Sign in at [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) with GitHub and import this repo.
2. Add the two environment variables from `.env.example` with your Supabase values.
3. Click **Deploy**.
4. Back in Supabase, open **Authentication → URL Configuration** and set **Site URL** to your Vercel address (for example `https://life-planner.vercel.app`) so confirmation emails link back to the app.

### 4. Make confirmation links work on any device (recommended)

By default the "Confirm your email" link only logs you in if you open it in
the same browser you signed up in. To make it work everywhere (for example,
signing up on a laptop and tapping the link in the Gmail app on your phone):

1. In Supabase open **Authentication → Emails → Templates → Confirm signup**.
2. Replace the link in the message body with:

   ```html
   <a href="{{ .SiteURL }}/auth/callback?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&type=email">Confirm your email</a>
   ```

3. Click **Save**.

## How it's organised

| Path | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `src/app/page.tsx` | Opens the most recent board (or Home when there are none) |
| `src/app/home/` | Home page: account email, menu with Log out, to-do list, milestone, countdown, all boards |
| `src/app/task/[id]/` | A milestone task's page (notes, made with TipTap) |
| `src/lib/planner.ts` | Saving the to-do list, milestone, linked ticks and pasted pictures |
| `src/components/board/LinkMenu.tsx` | A text's Link > Add to menu |
| `src/app/MergeDialog.tsx` | Merge: picking the other board, the warning, and merging |
| `src/components/board/merge.ts` | Merging one board's things into another (new ids, placed to the right) |
| `src/app/TornPaper.tsx` | The torn paper behind a board's name on Home |
| `src/app/BoardPreview.tsx` | The blurred picture of a board on Home |
| `src/app/BoardList.tsx` | Board boxes (used by Home and Open?): switch, remove (trash), duplicate, restore, new |
| `src/app/OpenPanel.tsx` | The Open? box on a board |
| `src/app/board/[id]/` | A saved board (saves as you go, keeps presets) |
| `src/app/actions.ts` | Making a new board |
| `src/app/login/` | Log in and create account |
| `src/lib/pages.ts` | Shared bits for saved boards (trash time, setup check) |
| `src/lib/useAutosave.ts` | Saves a page a moment after each change, in order; never overwrites newer work saved from another tab |
| `src/lib/supabase/` | Connecting to Supabase (server, browser, login check) |
| `src/middleware.ts` | Keeps you logged in and protects every page |
| `src/components/board/Board.tsx` | The white board |
| `src/components/board/model.ts` | What a board is made of (circles, squares, texts, lines, pictures, presets) |
| `src/components/board/constants.ts` | Sizes, colours and limits |
| `src/components/board/TextEditor.tsx` | Typing text (lists, Tab, sizes, bold, colours) |
| `src/components/board/richText.ts` | Text with parts in different sizes, bold and colours |
| `src/components/board/snapping.ts` | Lining things up while dragging |
| `src/components/board/SideMenu.tsx` | The menu opened with m (presets) |
| `src/components/board/ToolIcon.tsx` | Icons for the phone toolbar |
| `src/components/board/LineMenu.tsx` | The box beside a clicked line (Time, Angle, Text) |
| `src/components/board/geometry.ts` | Maths for bent lines and selection boxes |
| `src/components/board/timeline.ts` | Works out the dates along a timeline |
| `src/components/board/input.ts` | Mouse wheel vs trackpad, straight-line snapping |
| `src/components/board/icons.tsx` | Small menu icons |

`npm run format` tidies the code into one consistent style.
